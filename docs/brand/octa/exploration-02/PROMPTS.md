# OCTA — exploração 02

Cinco estudos raster gerados com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, a partir das quatro referências fornecidas pelo usuário. São propostas para seleção e posterior redesenho vetorial; ainda não são SVGs oficiais. A identidade do aplicativo não foi alterada.

01 — Nó entrelaçado; 02 — Selo circular; 03 — Kraken angular; 04 — Triângulo fluido; 05 — O geométrico.

## Prompts usados

### 01

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE original polished logo concept presentation for OCTA, a premium intelligent multichannel SaaS. The attached images are visual inspiration only, NOT edit targets; do not copy their exact logo. Learn from their integrated tentacle linework, interlacing and strong geometric silhouettes. Flat vector-like black/very dark forest green #152922 mark on solid warm off-white. No gradients, glass, texture, shadows, mockups, eyes, suckers, face, cartoon, flowers, AI sparkles, gear, hashtag or swastika shapes. Sophisticated controlled curves, generous negative spaces, simple reproducible geometry, recognizable independent silhouette. Square canvas with a large centered symbol filling about 55% canvas width, elegant clean uppercase geometric sans serif word OCTA centered below with generous letterspacing, one small model number at top left. Extremely crisp professional identity design. This is a raster concept intended for later vector refinement. DIRECTION 01 — INTERLACED KNOT. A compact upright octopus-derived continuous ribbon emblem. An arched central open head flows seamlessly into interwoven curling tentacles with 3 or 4 visible purposeful loop openings, balanced left and right but original configuration. Strong medium-thick monoline with round ends and precise over-under breaks. The silhouette is a rounded triangular knot, no external enclosing frame. More abstract and mature than a literal animal. Text only OCTA and 01.
